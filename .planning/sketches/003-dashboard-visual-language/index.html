About this dashboard

FIX-DASH supports the Region 3 biological-incident management review. It combines surveillance, vaccination, mortality and staffed-capacity evidence for operational coordination; it is not a patient-level clinical record or a substitute for statutory notifications.

Charts use labelled axes, text legends and redundant line or marker treatments. Keyboard focus remains visible, and status is never communicated by colour alone.

Methodology and operational definitions

Reporting uses notification date for case trends and admission date for capacity demand. Daily values close at 06:00 and remain labelled provisional until source reconciliation.

Confirmed case
A laboratory-confirmed record de-duplicated by person, specimen date and notifying facility.
Weekly incidence
Seven-day confirmed cases per 100,000 residents using the 2026 municipal population register.
Staffed capacity
Beds available with the staff and equipment needed for safe operation during the reporting shift.
Mortality attribution
Deaths where the incident pathogen is recorded as an underlying or contributing cause, pending final certification.
