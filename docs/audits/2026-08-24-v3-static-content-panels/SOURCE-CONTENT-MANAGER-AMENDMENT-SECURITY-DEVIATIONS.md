## GeoJSON pre-implementation calibration decision

The user does not choose numeric GeoJSON limits. The approved bounded disposable Chromium calibration ran after written amendment approval and before the final implementation plan. Its verified legitimate-project baseline covers `gemeente_2020.geojson`, `gemeente_2021.geojson`, `gemeente_2026.geojson`, and `netherlands-provinces.geojson`:

| Baseline maximum | Value | File |
|---|---:|---|
| Encoded bytes | 193,816 | `gemeente_2021.geojson` |
| Features | 355 | `gemeente_2020.geojson` |
| Total coordinate positions | approximately 6,630 | `gemeente_2021.geojson` |
| Positions in one feature | 196 | `netherlands-provinces.geojson` |

All four current files are shallow Polygon/MultiPolygon with flat properties, so they protect legitimate inputs but cannot alone determine caps. Disposable fixture ladders independently vary bytes, features, total coordinates, one-feature density, parts/rings, property-key/value volume, nesting/GeometryCollection depth, accepted geometry types, and concurrent active maps.

Actual Chromium exercises cover upload/read, parse/validation/summary, manager preview, replacement compatibility, persistence/reload, map register/render, pan/zoom/resize, and package export/import at Build 1440×900 and 1024×768 plus a pinned constrained device/CPU-memory profile. Evidence records per-phase median/p95 latency, main-thread long tasks, first usable preview/map, interaction responsiveness, memory/serialized footprint, and rollback.

Separate warning and hard resource/nesting caps are selected from observed performance/memory knees with margin above every legitimate fixture; bytes alone are insufficient. Join compatibility/coverage remains a replacement result, not a resource limit. The checker is itself bounded against adversarial nesting/complexity. Below warning accepts normally; the measured safe range between warning and hard cap warns and may proceed; above a hard cap rejects before commit.

Master review did not accept calibration commit `526003d` because its byte/property and part/ring hard caps preceded measured knees, the node budget was not numeric, and property-key semantics conflicted. Renewed review of `3f97ff9` accepted the latter three corrections but required meaningful byte/property margin below the 48 MB failure. One fresh constrained 36 MB encoded rung completed but reached the exact 2,000 ms hard knee. The final security limits therefore warn encoded source and encoded property-value bytes at 32,000,000 and hard-reject at 36,000,000, retaining 12 MB/25% below the 48 MB resource failure; geometry parts and polygon rings warn at 2,000 and hard-reject at 4,000; whole-document structural object/array nodes warn at 30,000 and hard-reject at 50,000; maximum own keys on one Feature.properties object warn at 512 and hard-reject at 1,000. An 8 MB source remains normal.

One proposed validation authority owns the corrected calibrated constants and exact metrics. `docs/audits/2026-08-24-v3-static-content-panels/GEOJSON-LIMITS-DECISION.md` records corpus facts, fixtures, environment, measurements, knees, margins, selected limits, GeometryCollection incompatibility, rollback, and both master corrections. No legitimate fixture is excluded and no material user-level UX tradeoff was found; the master accepted the guardrail at `c28b59d`. No production task/test may encode guessed or alternate values.

## Preserved boundaries

The amendment does not change these accepted constraints:

- QMD is native and portable; Google Docs integration is deferred.
- Arbitrary authored text remains accepted and inert; no sanitizer is introduced.
- SVG, APNG, animated WebP, active markup, mismatched MIME, unsafe local paths, and arbitrary CSS remain rejected or inert.
- Unsaved drafts remain application-session-only.
- Static content remains outside Chrono Groups and Scenes.
- Free text remains excluded from Present/Audience; standalone Image remains the only static Present/Audience type.
- The six-stage Add chart and four-stage Add static content workflows remain exact.
